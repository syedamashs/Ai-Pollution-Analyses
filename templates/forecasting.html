📊 Model Information

🧠 LSTM Neural Network

Long Short-Term Memory neural network captures complex temporal patterns and non-linear relationships in AQI data. Best for capturing sudden changes in air quality trends.

📉 ARIMA Statistical

AutoRegressive Integrated Moving Average model (statistical approach) works well for stationary time series data. Effective for linear trends and seasonal patterns.

🔮 Prophet (Meta)

Facebook's Prophet handles trend changes and seasonality robustly. Excellent for weekly cyclical patterns in pollution data and automatic anomaly detection.

🎯 Ensemble Average

Combines predictions from all three models to reduce individual model bias and increase robustness. Often provides more reliable forecast than any single model.
