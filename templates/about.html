About GreenMadurai

AI-Powered Environmental Monitoring and Tree Recommendation System for Madurai

🌍 Project Overview

GreenMadurai is an innovative AI-powered platform designed to address environmental challenges in Madurai city. By combining real-time air quality monitoring with advanced satellite image analysis, we provide actionable insights for regional greening initiatives.

Our system helps city planners, environmentalists, and community leaders make data-driven decisions about tree plantation and pollution management across Madurai, Chennai, Coimbatore, Dindigul, and Trichy.

✨ Key Features

📊 Real-time AQI Monitoring

Monitor air quality index across 5 key areas in Madurai with live data updates. View historical trends to understand pollution patterns over time.

🛰️ Satellite Image Analysis

AI-powered image segmentation identifies free land areas suitable for plantation. Get precise estimates of plantation capacity and tree counts.

🌳 Smart Tree Recommendations

Receive AI-suggested trees based on pollution levels. Each recommendation includes species details and pollution absorption capacity.

📈 Data-Driven Insights

Analyze trends, compare areas, and identify optimal zones for environmental interventions with comprehensive dashboards.
